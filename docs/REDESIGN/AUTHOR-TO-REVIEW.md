## Waiting on you

**Whom a push service is told to write to about your gateway.** Each notification goes through the push service of the browser it is for: Apple's, Google's or Mozilla's. The service can't read it, and with it the gateway says whom the service may write to. As built, that is the address of the page's door, where it is served over HTTPS, and on a tailnet that address holds your tailnet's name. It could be an address you set, such as an email, or nobody. Apple's documentation says its service turns away a contact that isn't a URL or a mailto address, and it took the page's address on 2026-11-23, when notifications reached your phone. Whether it takes a notification that names nobody is untried. You left it open on 2026-11-22: "not sure. we'll figure it out".
