Status: todo
Priority: P1

## Why
Scheduled and steward turns need awareness of active or stale work without loading full transcripts. A compact session-status item can point agents toward the right channels, owners, and recent outcomes.

## Build

- Include latest activity, likely owner/channel, blocked/running/stale hints, and recent task outcomes.
- Keep output concise and inspectable through existing or new CLI surfaces.

## Boundaries
- Do not replay raw transcripts.
- Do not create a second scheduler or heartbeat control plane.
